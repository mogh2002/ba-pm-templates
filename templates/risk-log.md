**Score** = likelihood × impact. Review the 7–9s every week, the 4–6s every two weeks.

**Response types:** avoid (change the plan) · reduce (lower the chance or effect) · transfer (someone else carries it) · accept (watch it, with a trigger).

Write risks as "if X happens, then Y": "If the client sends test data late, then UAT moves by a week." Not just "test data".

When a risk happens, it becomes an **issue**: move it to the status report with an owner and a date.
